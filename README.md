# Webleads

Website for Webleads: a front page for all services, and a page per service under `/services/`. The website offer (three packages from 2.995 kr.) is the old one-page site, now at `/services/hjemmesider`. Built from the Claude Design handoff "Hjemmeside v8".

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no build step on Vercel. The front page and the service pages are generated by a small script (see Pages and services). Hosted on Vercel.

The copy for the front page and the service pages is in `scripts/services/data.mjs`. After a change:

```
node scripts/services/build.mjs    # writes index.html and services/<slug>/index.html
node scripts/services/og.mjs       # share images in assets/og/ (needs Playwright)
```

Do not edit the generated files by hand; the next build overwrites them. `/services/hjemmesider` is edited by hand, except the list of services at the bottom (between `sitemap:start` and `sitemap:end`) and the mega menu (`mega:start`/`mega:end`), which the build refreshes. A new service: add a card to `services`, a page to `pages`, its slug to one of the `groups` and a line to `short` in `data.mjs`, then build, make the share image and add it to `sitemap.xml`.

- **Mega menu** ("Services" in the menu on every page; `lib.mega`, `assets/js/mega.js`, styles in main.css): the services in six groups (`groups` in data.mjs, four columns), each with a short line (`short`), the three most chosen for the visitor's segment, a way to a fixed price and a link to `/services`. It opens on hover with a mouse and on click or Enter everywhere; Escape, a click outside or leaving it closes it. On phones the button is ☰ next to Kontakt and the menu is a sheet. GA4: `menu_open` (`via` hover/klik, once a page); the links are `nav_click`/`cta_click` with `button` "Menu: …".
- **Services on the front page** (`lib.frontServices`): the order is `front.featured` and then the groups (or `segments.order` once the visitor has chosen). The first three show, the next row is half visible under a soft blur, and "Vis flere" opens the rest; the cut follows the layout (main.js → `data-fsv`). "+ Tilføj" works as before.
- **"Til …"** under the promises (`lib.trades`, `front.trades`): the label and the words in the band follow the visitor's segment (servicefag for Ydelser, kinds of webshops for Produkter online, both before a choice).

Prices: only the ones we sell at. Hjemmesider: Simpel one page 2.995 kr., Normal hjemmeside 9.995 kr., SEO hjemmeside 29.995 kr. Advertising (Google Ads, Meta Ads, Google Shopping Ads): 8.000 kr./md. for the first channel and 5.000 kr./md. for each extra. SEO and Lokal SEO 995 kr. i timen. Automations fra 4.999 kr. + 499 kr./md. Interne systemer fra 14.999 kr. All ex. VAT. A service with `price: null` says "Fast pris efter en snak"; that is every service added on 5 Nov 2026 except Lokal SEO, and ChatGPT Ads and Tracking (added 7 Nov 2026), until Mikkel sets their prices (the new ad channels too: LinkedIn, TikTok, Snapchat, Pinterest, YouTube, Bing, Reddit and ChatGPT). Never invent numbers, results or customers in the copy.

- On the cards, "+ Tilføj" puts the service in the contact form ("Du har valgt"), as the add-ons do on the website page. On a service page the form starts with that service (`data-base` on the form; `data-value` is its start price, used as the value of the Meta lead). With nothing chosen on the front page the e-mail says "Generel henvendelse".
- Old links to the website offer on the front page (`/#saadan`, `/#pris`, `/#tilvalg`, `/#video`) are sent on to `/services/hjemmesider` with the same `?utm_…` and `#…` (script at the top of `index.html`). `/#kontakt`, `/#teamet`, `/#faq` and `/#bestil` stay on the front page.
- `/services` is the page with every service (`scripts/services/pages/services.mjs`, styles `assets/css/alle-services.css`). `/services/SEO` and other paths with capitals are sent on to the lower-case page by `404.html` (a redirect in vercel.json would ignore case and loop).
- The intro curtain is shown on the first page of a visit only (sessionStorage `wl-intro`, class `intro-seen` set in `<head>`); the hero then animates in at once.
- Ads and posts about websites should link to `/services/hjemmesider`, not the front page.

## Contact form

The form posts to `/api/contact`, which emails the message to us through [Resend](https://resend.com) and sends the visitor a short receipt (with a link to `/bestil`). The mail code is shared with "Bestil en hjemmeside" in `api/_mail.js`. Set these in Vercel → Project → Settings → Environment Variables:

| Variable         | Required | Default                        |
|------------------|----------|--------------------------------|
| `RESEND_API_KEY` | yes      |                                |
| `CONTACT_TO`     | no       | `hej@webleads.dk`                 |
| `CONTACT_FROM`   | no       | `Webleads <hej@webleads.dk>`      |

### Thank-you page (`/tak/<side>`)

When the message has gone through, the visitor is sent to `/tak/<the page the form was on>`: `/tak/forside`, `/tak/resultater` or `/tak/<service slug>` (for instance `/tak/meta-ads`, `/tak/seo`, `/tak/hjemmesider`). It is one page, `tak/index.html` (`scripts/services/pages/tak.mjs`, `node scripts/services/build.mjs tak`): vercel.json serves it for every `/tak/<side>` and keeps the address, so a new page with the form gets its own thank-you address by itself. `assets/js/show/tak.js` puts the name of the service in from the address ("Din besked om Meta Ads"), from the list of services in `data.mjs`. Not indexed (robots meta and `X-Robots-Tag`), not in the sitemap. The e-mail to us says which page the form was sent from (`Side:`).

The lead is counted on the thank-you page, so every lead has an address that says where it came from:

- `URL contains /tak` is every lead from the contact form; `URL contains /tak/meta-ads` the ones from the Meta Ads page. That works in GA4, Meta (custom conversions) and Google Ads.
- `assets/js/show/tak.js` sends `generate_lead` to GA4 (`form_page` = the page, plus `selected`, `value`, `addons_total`, `receipt`) and `Lead` to Meta, only right after a real submit (a note in sessionStorage that the form leaves and the page removes). A reload or a direct visit is only a page view. Without sessionStorage the form page counts the lead itself, as before.
- Meta gets the Lead twice with the same event id (browser and `/api/contact`), both with the thank-you address as the page, so Meta counts it once.
- Which ad or campaign the visitor came from is not in the address: GA4, Meta and Google Ads know that from the click itself. Count leads from Meta ads in Meta, and leads per page with the address.
- "Bestil en hjemmeside" (`/bestil`) keeps its own flow (the draft is shown at once) and counts its lead there (`generate_lead` with `form_name` bestil).

Without a key, the form shows a message asking the visitor to email instead. Mail goes out from `hej@webleads.dk`, which Resend only accepts once `webleads.dk` is verified there (Resend → Domains, DNS records at Simply.com). Until then, mail to us is resent from Resend's test sender `onboarding@resend.dev` (it can only deliver to the address on the Resend account), and receipts to visitors are skipped and logged. The receipt never repeats the visitor's message, and greets by first name only, so the form cannot be used to send other text to someone else's inbox.

## Segments: "Hvad sælger du?"

A thin bar above the menu on every generated page and on `/services/hjemmesider` asks what the visitor sells: **Ydelser** or **Produkter online** (webshop). The answer is `html[data-seg]` (`ydelser` or `webshop`; missing until the visitor has chosen, which shows the neutral page). Settings and copy are in `scripts/services/data.mjs` → `segments`.

- **How it is set:** only by the visitor's own choice in the tabs (or the floating card). No links and no guessing from the page (Mikkel: we must be sure). Nothing is chosen in advance. The choice is kept in localStorage (`wl-seg`) and set before the page is drawn on the next pages (`lib.SEG_HEAD` in `<head>`).
- **Until a choice is made** the visitor is asked: a dot pulses by the question, a light sweeps over the tabs and an orange bubble under them says "Vælg her, så viser vi det, der passer til dig." (`segments.nudge`; on at most 3 pages a visit, gone after 10 seconds, ✕ stops it; not on phones, where it would cover the menu). A floating bar at the bottom asks the same after the first scroll or a few seconds (✕ hides it for the visit; it waits while the cookie question is open). On a computer it stays after the choice as a second set of tabs that shows the choice and can change it (Mikkel, 7 Nov 2026); on phones it goes when the choice is made. It steps aside while the contact form is on screen and while the mega menu is open.
- **What changes** (`assets/js/segment.js`): the hero on the front page (badge words and lead, `segments.front`), the order of the services in every list (`segments.order`, numbered again), the "Mest valgt" in the mega menu and the "Til …" band on the front page (segment.js sends a `wl:seg` DOM event that main.js listens to). Anything else can be shown for one segment with `data-seg-only="ydelser"`, `"webshop"` or `"neutral"`; main.css hides the rest.
- **GA4**: every event carries `segment` (`ydelser`, `webshop` or `ukendt`), so every report can be split by it, leads included. `segment_choice` (`segment`, `previous`, `segment_source` faner/bund, `nudge` ja/nej) logs each choice, and `segment_nudge` (`action` vist/lukket/skjult, `nudge_type` boble/bund) the bubble and the card. Register `segment`, `previous`, `segment_source`, `nudge`, `action` and `nudge_type` under Admin → Custom definitions (event scope).
- `/services/hjemmesider` is edited by hand; the build keeps its tabs and head script (between `seg-head:start`/`seg-bar:start` markers) and its main.css/main.js versions in step.

## Analytics (GA4, first-party)

`assets/js/analytics.js` batches events and posts them to `/api/collect` (`api/collect.js`), which forwards them to Google Analytics 4 with the Measurement Protocol. No Google script runs in the browser, and nothing is stored on the visitor's device: the client id is a hash of IP + user agent + the current day.

Set in Vercel → Settings → Environment Variables, then redeploy:

| Variable              | Required | Where to find it |
|-----------------------|----------|------------------|
| `GA4_MEASUREMENT_ID`  | no       | Defaults to `G-RMB6GL0B9C`, the webleads.dk stream |
| `GA4_API_SECRET`      | yes      | Same stream → Measurement Protocol API secrets → Create |
| `GA4_DEBUG`           | no       | `1` sends to GA4's validation endpoint and logs the result |

Without the API secret the endpoint accepts events and drops them. Visitors with Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track switched on send nothing. Keep `privatliv/index.html` in step if you change what is collected. A session is the same visitor in the same clock hour (set by `api/collect.js`), so the pages of a visit belong together without cookies. Only events on the list in `api/collect.js` are passed on; add new ones there.

**Debug:** open any page with `?wl_debug=1`. Every event is logged in the browser console and shows up live in GA4 → Admin → DebugView.

## Meta Pixel and Conversions API (only with consent)

`assets/js/meta-pixel.js` asks the visitor once (a small box, bottom left) whether we may use Meta Pixel `2323320305173396`. Nothing is loaded from Meta and no cookies are set until they say yes. The answer is kept in `localStorage` (`wl-meta-consent`); any element with `data-consent-open` (the "Cookies" link in the footer, the button in the privacy policy) opens the question again, and saying no revokes the pixel and deletes `_fbp`/`_fbc`. Visitors with Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track are not asked.

Every event is sent twice with the same `event_id`, so Meta counts it once: from the browser (`fbq`) and from our server through `/api/meta` (`api/meta.js` → `api/_meta.js`, the Conversions API). The Lead is sent by `/api/contact` instead, with the e-mail and name hashed (SHA-256). The message itself is never sent to Meta.

| Event | When |
|---|---|
| `PageView` | Every page view |
| `ViewContent` | A pop-up opens (`content_name` = pop-up key, e.g. `addon-seo`) |
| `CustomizeProduct` / `RemoveAddon` | An add-on is added to or removed from the package |
| `Lead` | The contact form is sent (`content_name` = chosen package, `value` = start prices, monthly add-ons count one month, `DKK`) |
| `Contact` | The floating sms/mail button, `sms:`, `mailto:` and `tel:` links |
| `SectionView` | A section reaches the upper half of the screen (`section` = its id) |
| `Scroll` | 25, 50, 75 and 90 % of the page |
| `CTAClick` | Any element with `data-event` (`button` = its label) |
| `FormStart` / `FormError` | First focus in the contact form / sending failed |
| `EngagedVisit` | 30 s, 1, 2 and 5 minutes of active time |

The custom events come from `analytics.js` (`fromAnalytics`), so a new GA4 event can be passed on to Meta by adding it to `FROM_GA` in `meta-pixel.js`. Use `metaTrack(event, params)` for anything else. The pixel runs on the front page, the service pages and `/privatliv`; keep `privatliv/index.html` in step if you change what is sent.

Set in Vercel → Settings → Environment Variables, then redeploy:

| Variable | Required | Where to find it |
|---|---|---|
| `META_CAPI_TOKEN` | for the server copy | Events Manager → the pixel → Settings → Conversions API → Generate access token |
| `META_TEST_EVENT_CODE` | no | Events Manager → Test events; remove it again after testing |
| `META_PIXEL_ID` | no | Defaults to `2323320305173396` |

Without the token the browser pixel still works; only the server copy is skipped.

## Sådan virker det

A pinned section (480vh with motion) where scrolling drives five steps: 01 Du fortæller, 02 Vi bygger den (the Nordvik demo in `demo/` builds itself), 03 Du er online (the address is typed and the status goes to Live), 04 Kunden søger (an example search for the trade and town is typed) and 05 Du bliver fundet (the visitor's site and Google profile are marked, with a button to Bestil). Phones get the steps as numbers with only the current one written out, and the demo in a phone frame.

"Prøv med din egen virksomhed" at the top takes a trade (with the Bestil trades as suggestions), a town and a company name. ## Explainer-video

The website page (`/services/hjemmesider`) is ready for an explainer video that replaces "Før og efter". It is switched off until there is a video: the section `#video` in `services/hjemmesider/index.html` has `hidden`, and nothing is fetched while it does. To switch it on:

1. Put the files in `assets/video/`: `explainer.mp4` (H.264 and AAC, 1920 × 1080 or 1280 × 720, ideally under 30 MB) and `explainer.jpg` (a still for before it plays, 1600 × 900). For example:
   `ffmpeg -i input.mov -c:v libx264 -crf 23 -preset slow -vf scale=1920:-2 -c:a aac -b:a 128k -movflags +faststart assets/video/explainer.mp4`
   `ffmpeg -ss 3 -i assets/video/explainer.mp4 -frames:v 1 -q:v 3 -vf scale=1600:-2 assets/video/explainer.jpg`
2. In `services/hjemmesider/index.html`: remove `hidden` from `<section class="vid-sec" …>`, set `data-varighed` to the length (e.g. `1:30`, shown on the play button), and add `hidden` to `<section class="ba-sec" …>` ("Før og efter") or delete it.
3. Optional: Danish subtitles as `assets/video/explainer.da.vtt`, and the `<track>` line in the section un-commented. A vertical video needs `--ar: 9 / 16` on `.vid`.

The video loads and plays on click only (with the browser's own controls after that). Start, 25/50/75 % and the end are tracked (see Tracking).

## Customer drafts (udkast)

Drafts of a website made for one prospective customer, shared by direct link only:
`webleads.dk/<slug>-forside`, `-ydelser`, `-om-os` and `-kontakt` (e.g. `/holms-maler-aps-forside`).
They are not linked anywhere, not in the sitemap, and send `noindex` both as a meta tag and as an
`X-Robots-Tag` header. Opening one sends a page view to GA4, so you can see when the customer looked. Meta Pixel never runs on drafts.

The front page is built for trades: hero with trade + town, trust strip, services, about, projects gallery, proof (rating, numbers, memberships, reviews), process, tax deduction, areas, FAQ and a contact form. It is SEO-ready for launch: title and meta description with trade + town, one H1, labelled images, and JSON-LD for the business (trade-specific type such as `HousePainter`, `Plumber`, `Electrician`) and the FAQ. Remove `noindex` and add `"domaene"` to the customer file when the site goes live.

```
api/_lib/udkast/brancher/<branche>.json   Trade templates: services, prices, FAQ, steps, hours, button wording (generisk = "Andet")
api/_lib/udkast/skabelon/                 The page templates
api/_lib/udkast/render.js                 Renders one page from a trade template + customer data (shared with api/udkast.js)
scripts/udkast-kunder/<slug>.json         One file per customer: name, town, nearby areas, phone, colour, overrides
scripts/nyt-udkast.mjs                    Builds udkast/<slug>/*.html from the two files above
udkast/_faelles/                          Shared CSS, JS and example photos for all drafts
udkast/<slug>/                            The generated draft (plain HTML, can be edited by hand)
```

Make a new draft:

1. Copy `scripts/udkast-kunder/holms-maler-aps.json`, set `branche` to one of the files in `api/_lib/udkast/brancher/` and fill in the customer's details.
2. Any field from the trade file can be overridden in the customer file, e.g. its own `ydelser` or `overskrift`.
   Set `"design"` to `1` (Klassisk), `4` (Mosaik: white tiles on light grey) or `5` (Minimal: thin lines, numbered lists, timeline). Default is 1. All designs are light.
   Set `"farver"` to three hex colours to choose what the colour picker in the draft bar offers.
   Or use a colour theme from `assets/js/temaer.js`: `"tema": "skov"` (main colour, accent and background), or `"tema": "eget"` with `"farve"` and `"accent"`; `"tone"` (`lys`, `varm`, `kolig`, `tonet`) sets the background.
   Example photos: give the trade file `"billeder": [{ "hero": "…", "om": "…", "hold": "…", "galleri": ["…"] }]` (one object per set, `"billedsaet"` picks one; a set without `galleri` uses the first set's). Without them the drafts show soft colour fields saying where the customer's own photos go. Every photo is marked "Eksempel på billede".
   All trades except `generisk` have two sets, made with Higgsfield, in `udkast/_faelles/billeder/<branche>/`: `hero-1/2` (4:5), `om-1/2` (4:3) and one `projekt-N` per entry in `projekter` (same order as the captions). Each photo is there as `<navn>.webp` (896 px wide for hero, 960 px for the rest) and `<navn>-480.webp`; the renderer adds `srcset`/`sizes` for photos in that folder. No faces, text or logos in them.
   Trades with a `"garanti"` (maler, tømrer, el, VVS) promise it in the title and the proof numbers; the others lead with their first trust point and show the number of people.
3. Run `node scripts/nyt-udkast.mjs scripts/udkast-kunder/<slug>.json` and commit `udkast/<slug>/`.

`scripts/` is listed in `.vercelignore`, so customer files are never published. The URL pattern lives in `vercel.json` (rewrite plus headers); add a page name there if a draft needs more pages.

## Bestil en hjemmeside (free instant draft)

`assets/js/bestil.js` + `assets/css/bestil.css`: a full-screen flow in five steps (trade, company, what the site should do, design and colour, contact), then the visitor's own draft right away. It opens from any `[data-bestil]` link (hero, price, contact), on `/#bestil`, and as its own page at `/bestil` (`bestil/index.html`, for ads). The answers are kept in `sessionStorage` until sent, so a reload does not lose them.

- `api/bestil.js` checks the answers, e-mails them to `CONTACT_TO` with a link to the draft, sends the Lead to Meta (with consent), and returns the link. The visitor also gets the link by e-mail once `webleads.dk` is verified in Resend (see Contact form); the result screen says so only when that mail went out. Without `RESEND_API_KEY` the visitor still gets the draft, but the order is only in the function log.
- `api/udkast.js` renders the draft at `/mit-udkast?d=…` (and `/mit-udkast/ydelser|om-os|kontakt?d=…`). The answers about the business are in `d` (base64url JSON, see `api/_lib/udkast/bestilling.js`); nothing is stored, and the link holds no personal contact details. Unknown details get the same placeholders as hand-made drafts (phone 12 34 56 78, CVR 12345678). Pages send `noindex`.
- Choices change the draft: online booking gives a "Book tid" button, without "Priser" the prices are left out, and the sections for pictures and reviews are hidden unless chosen (when nothing is chosen, everything is shown).
- Colours are themes (`assets/js/temaer.js`): a main colour, an accent and a background tone. Step 4 shows the four themes for the trade, "Flere farver" shows all, and "Lav dine egne farver" lets the visitor pick their own two colours. The draft computes readable text versions of both colours, so own colours stay legible.
- Step 5 asks how (ring, sms, mail) and when the visitor wants to be contacted; it goes in our e-mail, the receipt and "Hvad sker der nu?". Ring and sms need a phone number.
- The result is presented as a first sketch, not a finished site: it says we get in touch within a few hours on weekdays and make a finished draft with their own photos, texts and wishes. There the visitor can change design, theme and background, open a new suggestion ("Vis et nyt forslag", or steered with "Mere farve", "Mere enkel", "Mere moderne", "Mere klassisk", "Anden tekst" — each trade has three headlines — and "Andre billeder", which switches to the trade's other set of example photos; not shown for "Andet"), go back and forth between suggestions, keep favourites, and send us their wishes: `api/oensker.js` e-mails the text, the buttons they pressed and links to the suggestion on screen and the favourites. Inside the result the draft's own bar is hidden; opened on its own, the bar offers three themes as links.
- Old links (with only one colour, `c`) still show the draft as before.
- Tracking: GA4 `bestil_open` (`from`), `bestil_step` (`step`, `name`), `bestil_close` (`step`) and `generate_lead` (`selected` = "Bestil: <fag>"), plus the result events in Tracking above; Meta `InitiateCheckout`, `BestilStep` and `Lead` (3.000 kr.).
- The three design pictures in step 4 are `assets/bestil/design-1|4|5.jpg` (screenshots of a draft in the "Kul og messing" theme at 1280 × 800, 640 px wide; bump `?v=` in `bestil.js` when they change).

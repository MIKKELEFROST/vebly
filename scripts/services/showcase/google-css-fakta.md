# Google CSS: facts and wording (checked 4 Nov 2026, updated 5 Nov 2026)

Two researchers and two independent verifiers checked these against Google's own help pages and the market.
Follow them when you write about Google CSS anywhere on the site (the page, the cards, the pop-ups, ads, posts).

## Webleads Shopping

- Webleads has its own CSS: **Webleads Shopping**, a price comparison site at https://shopping.webleads.dk
  (Mikkel, 5 Nov 2026). It is **free** (Mikkel, 9 Nov 2026): no subscription and no fee per click; we earn on the price comparison, not on the CSS. The ad budget is still paid to Google as today.
- Write it as »vores egen CSS, Webleads Shopping« or »Webleads Shopping«. In ad mockups the label is
  »Af Webleads Shopping«.
- No binding terms have been set: never write »Ingen binding« (or any binding) until Mikkel sets one.

## How it works (say it like this)

- In Denmark, Shopping ads on Google are always placed through a CSS (comparison shopping service). If the
  shop has not chosen another one, it is Google's own CSS, Google Shopping.
- Google Shopping is itself a CSS and bids in the same auction as the other CSSs.
- **Mechanism (Google's own framing):** Google's own CSS **deducts a fixed percentage margin from the
  shop's bid before the bid enters the auction**. With another CSS the **whole bid counts**. So the same click
  can become cheaper, or the shop can get more clicks for the same budget, or a bit of both. The auction still
  sets the price, and nothing is guaranteed.
  - Danish line to use: »Googles egen CSS trækker en margin fra dit bud, før det kommer med i auktionen.
    Med Webleads Shopping tæller hele buddet.«
  - Do NOT say »lægger en margin oven i klikprisen«.
- **The 20% is an industry estimate.** Google does not publish the margin. Use »op til 20%« and keep this
  sentence on the same screen as the number: »Google oplyser ikke marginens størrelse; 20% er branchens skøn.«
  Where there is no room for the sentence (a ball pop-up, a chip, a sticker), leave the number out.

## The ad label

- Every Shopping ad in Denmark has a small link as the last line of the card, under shop name, price,
  delivery and rating: »Af Google« for Google Shopping, otherwise »Af [CSS-navn]«. For us: »Af Webleads
  Shopping«. Image, title, price and shop name look the same; that line is the only visible difference.

## The calculator on /services/google-css

1. »Flere klik for samme budget« (the example numbers, 2.000 → 2.500 klik at 5,00 → 4,00 kr., show the
   effect). If a percentage is shown at all, the only correct form is »op til ca. 25% mere budkraft for samme bud«.
2. The fine print stays next to the numbers: »Eksempel: klikpris 5,00 kr. uden og 4,00 kr. med Webleads
   Shopping, hvis margenen hos Googles egen CSS er ca. 20%. Google oplyser ikke marginens størrelse; 20% er
   branchens skøn. Hvor meget du får ud af det, afhænger af auktionen.«
3. Example click prices 5,00 kr. → 4,00 kr. are fine (Danish Shopping CPCs are typically about 1–8 kr.).

## Never show or say

- »rabat«, »Spar 20%«, »garanteret«, »Google giver dig 20%«, »Google tager 20%«, »EU-godkendt«.
- Free listings, »gratis visninger« or a »populære produkter« block (removed from EEA search in Sept. 2026).
- A »Shopping« tab in a mock tab row (its status in the EEA after Sept. 2026 is disputed). Use Alle,
  Billeder, Videoer, Nyheder.
- »Uden kampagnestop«, »sker med det samme«, »på 60 minutter«, »Ingen binding«.
- Other real CSS providers, real shops, any Google logo.
